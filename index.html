Calculation & editing notes

The workspace 5′ selector offers H (5′-OH) or DMT (5′-O-DMT). The 3′ terminus remains OH. DMT changes the primary strand mass; the generated complement has free OH termini. The 2′ position depends on sugar chemistry. Empty strands are not molecules. Calculations exclude hydrates, free ions, salts and mass-spectrometry adducts.

Insert left of a linkage to add PO on the new nucleotide's left; insert right to add PO on its right. Deleting a nucleotide removes both adjacent linkages and reconnects new neighbors with PO. Moving means deleting, then inserting. Strands run 5′ → 3′.

Use prefixed tokens such as dA, rU, mC and fG, joined by - or -(s)-. Bare sequences such as ACGT are not inferred. Recognized incomplete drawings, such as dA-(s)-, remain editable. Open ends and adjacent linkages are marked red; molecular properties show Invalid until the strand is complete. Unrecognized text cannot be drawn.

Average masses use fixed conventional atomic weights; monoisotopic masses use NIST isotope masses. Decimal places are a display setting, not experimental precision. Full modified-sequence calibration against professional software is pending.
